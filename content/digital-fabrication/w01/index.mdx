<Entry date="TBD" title="Exploration · A Bending Butterfly">

A paper ring found on Pinterest inspired the bending form. I also explored [Trotec’s kerf-cut patterns](https://www.troteclaser.com/en/helpcenter/materials/application-techniques/bending-technique).

I first wanted to make a butterfly ring with crossing wings, bending a flat wooden band into a full circle. I tried several versions, but even the most flexible version could not bend all the way around. The wood did not allow enough movement for the form I wanted.

<Entry date="TBD" title="Revision · Interlocking Pieces">

Slotted wooden forms found on Pinterest inspired the interlocking construction. I changed from bending to assembly. Separate slotted pieces form a three-dimensional heart above the finger ring, using the wood as rigid panels instead of forcing it into a tight curve.
