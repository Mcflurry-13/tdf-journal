<Entry date="TBD" title="Concept · Word Play">

I wanted each of the four letter rings to rotate independently, so I could line up letters to make words or discover unexpected combinations. I measured my finger before modeling the rings; a clip holds the stack together and frames the selected letters.

<Entry date="TBD" title="Iteration · Lettering">

The first letters did not cut through the ring wall and were difficult to read in the print. I revised them into cutouts to make the lettering more distinct, while keeping the basic ring form.

<Entry date="TBD" title="Iteration · Clip Clearance">

The clip matched the ring dimensions in CAD, but the printed fit was too tight: turning the rings broke the clip. I widened it to leave room for movement. Printing itself went smoothly; the main revisions were letter legibility and the clearance needed between moving parts.
