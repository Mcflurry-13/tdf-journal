<Entry date="2026-09-20" title="From a family collection to an interaction">

My grandfather collects ceramic crafts, and our home is filled with them. Quiet, fragile and still, they can become easy to overlook. I wanted interaction to bring these objects back into attention and invite associations with family and memory.

A visitor's gesture becomes a projected pattern on a turning vessel. The axisymmetric form keeps its outline consistent as it rotates.

<Entry date="2026-09-20" dateEnd="2026-09-24" title="Iterating the servo mount">

I printed about four mount variants. The early pocket trapped the cable; opening the side helped insertion but reduced support. I revised the walls and added a top-open cable slot so the servo and its wires could lower into place together.

<Entry date="2026-09-22" dateEnd="2026-09-24" title="Supporting stable bearing rotation">

The open base let me inspect how the tray was supported. I arranged three upper bearings with three corresponding vertical bearing supports below to guide rotation and keep the tray stable.

The brackets left too much sideways play. Adding spacers and stepped locating pins held the bearings in position while allowing them to rotate freely.

<Entry date="2026-09-22" dateEnd="2026-09-24" title="Fitting the gear to the servo horn">

The pinion's recess needs to fit the servo arm closely so they rotate together. The first printed pocket jammed at the sharp internal corners; adding rounded relief allowed the arm to seat properly.

I also checked the pinion against the tray gear during assembly, because the horn fit, gear spacing and height all affect engagement.

<Entry date="2026-09-24" dateEnd="2026-09-29" title="From presence to a deliberate brushstroke">

The first version tracked the torso and limbs to detect a person in view. I then switched to MediaPipe Holistic so face and hand tracking could work together, with p5.js drawing the marks.

Pinching the thumb and index finger puts the pen down; releasing lifts it. One welcome rotation per visit avoids repeated triggering while someone stays in frame.

I tried cloud motifs and Chinese ink-painting prompts, then simplified the decoration to leave more space around the visitor’s mark.
