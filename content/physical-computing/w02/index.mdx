<Entry date="2026-09-03" title="External LED · Blink and Print">

I moved the LED output onto a breadboard, synchronized it with the onboard LED, and printed each ON/OFF state in the Serial Monitor. The circuit follows D8 → 220 Ω resistor → LED → GND.

I initially pasted two sketches together and created duplicate setup() and loop() functions. I fixed this by combining both behaviors into one setup() and one loop().

<Entry date="2026-09-03" title="Light Input · LDR">

I used an LDR to turn the LED on in darkness and off in bright light. The LDR and 10 kΩ resistor form a voltage divider read by A0; D8 controls the LED through a 220 Ω resistor.

The reading first stayed around 1018–1023, so the LED never changed state. I checked the A0 midpoint and GND connection, then measured bright and dark values before choosing a threshold.

<Entry date="2026-09-03" title="Three-LED Sequence">

I connected red, yellow, and green LEDs to D8, D9, and D10 and turned them on one at a time. Each LED uses its own 220 Ω resistor, while all three share GND.

I aligned the components so wiring mistakes were easier to see, then changed delay() to test different flashing speeds.
